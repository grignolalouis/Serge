# Sourcing Strategy Review

## Metadata
- **Level:** L5
- **Use Case:** UC-3 (strategic sourcing)
- **Systems:** SRM
- **Expected tools:** 4–7
- **Realistic user:** Procurement director preparing a quarterly review

## Question
"We want to rethink our sourcing for Nam Doc Mai mangoes. Based on our data, which supplier should we rely on and why? What's the risk if we stop using the other one?"

## Required Facts

**Critical:**
- Two suppliers sell PROD-001: SUP-001 (Somchai, 280 THB) and SUP-004 (Rayong Tropical, 270 THB)
- SUP-001: rating 4.5, 3-day lead, 100% on-time in scenario POs (PO-001, PO-004, PO-006, PO-008 in transit)
- SUP-004: rating 4.0, 3-day lead, delivered PO-012 on time (Jan 22, 1 day early)
- Both are reliable; SUP-004 is cheaper (270 vs 280 THB = 10 THB/unit saving)

**Important:**
- SUP-001 has more volume/history in our data — better track record
- SUP-004 is a good secondary source (diversification)
- Risk of single-sourcing: if chosen supplier has a disruption, no fallback

**Nice to have:**
- Recommendation: **dual-source** (keep both) for resilience; primary based on preference
- Mention volumes shipped historically
- Observation that SUP-001 is currently shipping PO-008 (150 units) — indicating active relationship

## Forbidden Content
- Claim that only one supplier exists for PROD-001
- Invented cost or reliability figures

## Flexibility Notes
- This is a strategic question — the agent's recommendation matters but there's no single "right" answer
- The judge focuses on factual grounding + coherence of argument
- Either supplier can be recommended as primary, as long as the reasoning is sound

## Ground Truth

| Supplier | Cost/unit | Rating | Lead | Scenario reliability |
|----------|-----------|--------|------|----------------------|
| SUP-001 Somchai Mango Farm | 280 THB | 4.5 | 3d | 3/3 received on-time, 1 in transit |
| SUP-004 Rayong Tropical | 270 THB | 4.0 | 3d | 1/1 received on-time (early) |

Dual-sourcing is the risk-balanced answer; either can be primary.
